DEBUT GAME • MAN OF THE MATCH

SUJIN

A Dream Debut. A Complete Performance.

What a way to announce his arrival! In his very first match, Sujin delivered a phenomenal all-round performance and earned the title of Man of the Match. He found the net several times, showing impressive attacking instincts and the confidence to make a difference when his team needed him most.

Sujin was equally brilliant in possession, delivering accurate passes, linking up with teammates, and demonstrating outstanding teamwork. He didn't just focus on scoring; he created opportunities and helped keep the entire team connected on the pitch.

And when duty called at the other end, he stepped up with solid goalkeeping as well. From scoring goals to distributing the ball intelligently and protecting the goal, Sujin proved that his debut was about much more than individual brilliance. It was a statement of versatility, composure, and team spirit.
